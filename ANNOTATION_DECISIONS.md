## Staged workflow

1. Annotate 50 de-identified posts to find ontology problems; do not estimate performance yet.
2. Expand to approximately 150 posts after recording and resolving ambiguous cases.
3. Expand to 400–500 posts only after the codebook is stable, covering every subreddit family in the sampling manifest.
4. If a second annotator is available, double-code a preselected shared subset and calculate agreement before automation.
