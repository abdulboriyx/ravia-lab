# Cell Biology Limitation Register v1

- Manual/browser visual acceptance: `D_H_CELL_BIOLOGY_VISUAL_ACCEPTANCE_PENDING — UNVERIFIED — ENVIRONMENT BLOCKED`.
- Next.js production build: `UNVERIFIED — ENVIRONMENT BLOCKED` when Turbopack cannot spawn its worker/bind its internal port.
- Multipass membrane proteins remain explicitly deferred.
- Full ERAD and full lysosomal degradation remain explicitly deferred.
- Folding and organelle renderings are S2 schematic where applicable; no atomistic folding claim is made.
- D-series timelines encode deterministic teaching/mechanism ordering, not physical rates.
- Quantitative concentrations, dose-response, stochastic occupancy, diffusion, motor velocity, and ATP kinetics are not modeled.
- D-G target response is a bounded signaling prerequisite/reference; D-C remains transcription authority.
- Whole-cell relative scale is schematic and never scientific authority.
